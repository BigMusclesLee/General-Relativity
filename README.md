# General-Relativity
GitHub repository for my work in general relativity 

For my module, marking was split between a project and an exam with 40% and 60% weighting respectively.

This repository will only contain my project due to being unable to access my exam.

The project was split into two parts, one half on deriving equations and creating a model for a particle around a blackhole in Schwarzschild spacetime, and the other half on writing a scientific biography on a physicist with work in general relativity. I decided on Andrea Ghez, a female physicist who won the nobel prize in physics in 2020 becoming the fourth woman to ever do so.

20% Modelling half of project, 95/100

20% Biography half of project, 75/100

60% Exam, 81/100


For an overall grade of 82.60%
